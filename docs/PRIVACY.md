# Privacy Policy

## Data Collection
- Email address (authentication)
- Scanned URLs (anonymized after 30 days)
- Usage statistics (aggregated)

## Data Storage
- PostgreSQL (encrypted at rest)
- Hosted in Singapore region
- Backups retained for 30 days

## Data Sharing
- No data sold to third parties
- Anonymized for research only
- Threat data shared with community

## User Rights
- Right to access
- Right to deletion
- Right to data portability
- Right to opt-out
